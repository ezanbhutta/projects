Shipping

In-stock orders ship within 1 to 2 business days with tracked delivery. You'll get a confirmation email with tracking as soon as it's on the way.

  • Free shipping on orders over $75.
  • Below $75, a flat rate is shown at checkout before you pay.
  • We currently ship from the United States.
  • Pre-orders ship in 3 to 4 weeks; if an order mixes in-stock and pre-order items, it ships together when the pre-order is ready unless you ask us to split it.

Returns & exchanges

If it's not right, you have 30 days from delivery to return or exchange it.

  • Items should be unworn and in original condition.
  • Start a return by emailing hello@unello.example with your order number.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method once we receive the item.
  • Exchanges (e.g. a different size or tier) ship free.

Damaged or wrong item

If something arrives damaged or incorrect, email us within 14 days and we'll make it right, no return shipping on us.
