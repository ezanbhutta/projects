Our commitment

Unello is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Everyone should be able to learn the signal and shop, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

What we've built in

  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus rings on every interactive element.
  • Colour contrast of at least 4.5:1 for text, including text on the Sun and Rose signal colours.
  • Touch targets of at least 44×44px.
  • Labelled form fields, descriptive alt text, and semantic landmarks.
  • Respect for prefers-reduced-motion, the signal pulse and reveals stop, the static dot remains.
  • Content that reflows cleanly from 375px up to large screens.

The signal & colour

The signal is never communicated by colour alone in the interface, it's always paired with a label (e.g. "Social, Sun"), so the meaning is available to everyone.

Found a barrier?

Tell us at access@unello.example and we'll fix it. Accessibility is ongoing, not a checkbox.
