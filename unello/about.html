About

Unity, and hello.

Unello is two words pressed into one, and a small object with a big job: to make the first hello, in real life, a little easier.

Why it exists

More connected. Somehow lonelier.

We sit beside each other and never look up. Meeting people quietly moved behind a screen, and something human got lost in the move.

Unello puts a signal back into the real world. A bracelet that says a simple thing to anyone nearby who knows the code: I'm open. You can say hello.

The mission

Turn "I wish I'd said something" into "I did."
