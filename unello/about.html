About

Unity + Hello.

Unello is two words pressed into one: Unity and Hello. It's a small object with a big job — to make the first hello, in real life, a little easier.

Why it exists

We are more connected than ever and lonelier than ever. We sit beside each other and never look up. Meeting people — for friendship or for dating — has quietly moved behind a screen, and something human got lost in the move.

Unello puts a signal back into the real world. A bracelet or a ring, in one of two colours, says a simple thing to anyone nearby who knows the code: I'm open. You can say hello.

The founder

Unello started with Alicia — someone who moved cities one too many times and got tired of how hard it was to simply meet people. Apps felt like work. Bars felt like a gamble. The people she wanted to know were standing right there, in the same café, behind the same invisible wall.

So she made the wall a little lower. A piece of jewelry that does one honest thing: it tells the truth about whether you're open. No profile, no performance — just a colour, worn out loud.

The mission

Make saying hello easier. Turn "I wish I'd said something" into "I did." Build a shared, opt-in signal that works the same on a beach, in a market, or on a morning commute — and that always leaves the wearer in control.

A movement, not a store

If you leave understanding the signal but never buy a thing, we still did half our job — because the code only works when people know it. And if you wear one out into the world, you make the next person's hello easier too. That's the whole movement: one signal at a time.
