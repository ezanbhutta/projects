About

Unity + Hello.

Unello is two words pressed into one. A small object with a big job, to make the first hello, in real life, a little easier.

Why it exists

More connected. Somehow lonelier.

We sit beside each other and never look up. Meeting people, for friendship or for dating, quietly moved behind a screen, and something human got lost in the move.

Unello puts a signal back into the real world. A bracelet, in one of two colours, says a simple thing to anyone nearby who knows the code: I'm open. You can say hello.

The founder

It started with one too many new cities.

Unello began with Alicia, someone who moved one too many times and got tired of how hard it was to simply meet people. Apps felt like work. The people she wanted to know were standing right there, behind the same invisible wall.

So she made the wall a little lower: a piece of jewelry that does one honest thing, tell the truth about whether you're open. No profile, no performance. Just a colour, worn out loud.

The mission

Turn "I wish I'd said something" into "I did."

Build a shared, opt-in signal that works the same on a beach, in a market, or on a morning commute, and that always leaves the wearer in control. If you leave understanding the signal but never buy a thing, we still did half our job, because the code only works when people know it.
