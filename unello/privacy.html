What we collect

  • Contact details you give us — name, email, shipping address — to fulfil orders and answer questions.

How we use it

To ship your order, provide support, send updates you opted into, and improve the experience. We don't sell your personal data.

Email & marketing

If you join the movement, we'll email you about drops and news. You can unsubscribe any time from a link in every email.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the cart and checkout, and optional analytics cookies you can decline. A consent banner appears if tracking pixels are enabled.
