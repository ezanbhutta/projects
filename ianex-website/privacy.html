Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 June 2026 · Template for client review. Independent legal review recommended.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how IANEX Global Services ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information you provide through this website, primarily via our contact form. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and in line with applicable data protection requirements.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include your full name, email address, company name, and the contents of your message. We do not collect payment information through this website, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about your request
  • To arrange and follow up on a Discovery Consultation, if requested
  • To maintain a record of correspondence for legitimate business purposes

Booking & third-party services

Consultation bookings are handled through Calendly, which processes your booking information in accordance with its own privacy policy. This website may be hosted on a platform such as Wix, which can process limited technical data such as cookies and basic analytics to operate and secure the site.

Data retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as necessary to respond to your request and for reasonable business record-keeping, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@ianexglobal.com.

This policy is provided as a template covering data collected via the contact form. The client is advised to obtain independent legal review to ensure full compliance with all applicable regulations.
