01 — Ethos

Since 2014

We do not list property. We place legacy — the rare homes worth keeping quiet about.

Every residence we represent is chosen for one reason: it will still feel considered in fifty years. We work slowly, with a small circle of architects, owners, and clients who value discretion over spectacle.

No open houses. No billboards. A curated few homes, presented with the patience they deserve — and shown only to those we know can appreciate them.
